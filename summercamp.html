2026 Summer Programs

This summer, CodeBloom is bringing hands-on, play-based coding education to students in grades 3 through 12. Our programs are designed to be fun, engaging, and empowering, giving young people the tools and confidence to explore a future in tech! No prior coding experience required!

All programs take place on weekdays only. Need-based fee waivers are available, no questions asked. Email board@codebloom.org to request one before registering.

What to Expect

Hands-On Coding

Students learn by doing, working through fun challenges and real projects using Python and other tools!

Team Activities

Collaborative group projects help students build communication and problem-solving skills alongside their peers in a supportive environment.

Community & Mentorship

Participants connect with mentors and become part of the broader CodeBloom community, a network that supports them long after the program ends.

Play-Based Learning

We use games, puzzles, and creative activities to show students that coding is something they already understand, we just give it a new name!
